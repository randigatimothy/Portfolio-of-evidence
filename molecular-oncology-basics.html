Molecular Oncology

Molecular oncology, from a pharmacology starting point

28 Mar 2026

Pharmacology training gives you drug mechanisms as isolated facts: this kinase inhibitor blocks this target. Molecular oncology gives those same facts a home — the hallmarks of cancer framework, which explains why that target mattered to begin with.

Mapping drugs onto hallmarks

Working through the hallmarks — sustained proliferation, evading cell death, angiogenesis, and the rest — I found myself constantly translating back to drug classes I already knew from dispensing: tyrosine kinase inhibitors against sustained proliferative signalling, PARP inhibitors against genomic instability. Having the mechanism named gave those drugs a logic I'd been applying without fully understanding.

The gap this doesn't close

None of this touches why a patient does or doesn't take the drug once it's prescribed correctly for the right mechanism. That's a reminder that molecular precision and real-world adherence are separate problems, both of which matter, and my research interests still sit mostly in the second one.
