I trained and practised as a pharmacist before starting this MPhil, which means most of what I know about cancer treatment I learned from behind a dispensary counter — watching what patients actually did with their prescriptions, not just what the guidelines said they should do.

This research writing module asks for a portfolio of evidence, so this site is that: a running account of the notes I take through each module, the research I'm building toward a thesis, and the less structured reflections that don't fit neatly into either. It's written for my supervisors and examiners first, but also for the version of me that will want to remember how this actually felt.

Cancer science sits at the point where pharmacology, epidemiology, and health systems all have to agree with each other. That intersection — specifically in oncology pharmacy and cancer epidemiology — is where I'm trying to build a research identity.
