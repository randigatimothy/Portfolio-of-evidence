01

The question

Cancer treatment guidelines describe how a patient should take an anticancer medicine, and cancer registries describe what happened to patients at a population level — but very little sits between the two, showing how real adherence and access actually shape those outcomes. Six years of dispensing oral anticancer therapies gave me a strong clinical instinct for where that gap opens up; this research is an attempt to turn that instinct into a testable question, with my supervisor's guidance on scope and method.

02

Proposed approach

The current thinking combines routinely collected pharmacy dispensing records with population-level cancer registry data, using descriptive epidemiology first to characterise adherence patterns, before testing their association with recorded outcomes. This is deliberately framed as a starting point — the final design will depend on data access, ethics approval, and supervisor input, and this page will be updated as those constraints become clearer.
